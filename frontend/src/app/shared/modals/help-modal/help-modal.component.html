How Refleet takes a change from an idea to merge requests across your GitLab fleet.

Connecting GitLab

Under Settings, connect a group by providing its group path, an access token, and (if self-managed) your GitLab instance URL. Refleet syncs the group's projects into your fleet, and you can trigger a re-sync at any time with "Sync now" to pick up new or removed projects.

Qualifications and Shifts

Deciding where a change belongs and making it are separate steps. A qualification asks which projects a change applies to and answers it per project; a shift applies the change and opens the merge requests. Keeping them apart lets you check the verdict before anything writes to a repository, and reuse one qualification for any number of shifts.

Running a qualification

Qualify projects with a static regex match against a file (e.g. a dependency version in composer.json), or with an AI prompt when the condition can't be expressed as a pattern. A qualification goes Draft → Running → Completed, and each project ends up Qualified or Not Qualified. You can override any verdict by hand.

Running a shift

A shift takes its targets from a qualification's qualified projects, from an explicit selection, or from projects you pick directly without qualifying at all. Define the change — a file, pattern and replacement, or a prompt — and the shift goes Draft → Applying Change → Completed. Refleet keeps watching each merge request after opening it, so a target is only done once its merge request is merged or closed.

Runner Fleet

Runners are the workers that qualify and apply changes and open merge requests on your behalf. Each runner reports its status — working, idle, or offline — via a heartbeat, and you can see the whole runner fleet's health on the Runners page and the dashboard's runner status widget. Nothing progresses until at least one runner is connected.

Team & Roles

Invite teammates by email from the Team page. Every account is either the organization's owner or a regular member — the owner can invite and remove teammates, and can transfer ownership to another member at any time.

Support

If you need assistance or have questions about the platform, contact our support team. We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours.
