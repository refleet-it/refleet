# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to help.

## What that means here

Technical disagreement is the point of the project, and vigorous argument about a design is
welcome. What is not welcome: personal attacks, dismissiveness about someone's experience
level, harassment of any kind, and continuing to press a point after it has been decided.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space the project uses.

## If something goes wrong

Email **contact@refleet.it**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private.

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close threads, and block accounts, and will say
plainly when doing so and why.
